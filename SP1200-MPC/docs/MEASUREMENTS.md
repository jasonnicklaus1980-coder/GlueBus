# SP1200 2.0: measurements of the model

Generated by `make stagetest` (test/stage_test.cpp) at a 44.1 kHz host rate, default settings unless stated, noise sources off except in section 10. Hardware reference figures: owner's manual and spec sheet (26.04 kHz, 12-bit, S/N 90 dBA, THD 0.05 %), owners' filter measurements (Out 3-4 ~7.5 kHz, Out 5-6 ~10 kHz). No measurements of a physical unit were available to this build; the comparisons are with those published figures.

## 1. Clock and resolution

- DAC steps per second at host 44100 Hz: **25958** (hardware clock 26 040 Hz)
- DAC steps per second at host 48000 Hz: **25959** (hardware clock 26 040 Hz)
- DAC steps per second at host 96000 Hz: **25960** (hardware clock 26 040 Hz)
- 45>33 GRIT at -8 st: **16390** new values per second (sampling clock 16 400 Hz, DAC still 26 040)
- -80 dBFS sine: **1** distinct output levels (12-bit ideal: 1)
- -40 dBFS sine: **41** distinct output levels (12-bit ideal: 42)

## 2. Zero-order hold (Out 7-8, unfiltered)

| tone | measured | ideal sinc(f/26.04k) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 kHz | -0.02 dB | -0.02 dB |
| 5.0 kHz | -0.53 dB | -0.53 dB |
| 10.0 kHz | -2.22 dB | -2.22 dB |
| 12.5 kHz | -3.58 dB | -3.59 dB |

A 3 kHz tone's first image (23.04 kHz) is above the 44.1 kHz host's Nyquist. With raw host-grid steps it folds to 21.06 kHz at **-17.9 dB**; with band-limited steps (hardware model) it stays out: **-101.9 dB**.
An 8 kHz tone's image at 18.04 kHz is kept: **-8.7 dB** (the ZOH predicts -8.5 dB).

## 3. Sampling: what folds back

- 18 kHz input aliases to 8.04 kHz: **-3.4 dB** without the anti-alias filter, **-24.8 dB** with it (15 kHz, 4th order: leaky by design).
- 12 kHz tone: alias at 8 kHz **-2.5 dB** with SAMPLE RATE 20 kHz, **-173.7 dB** at 26.04 kHz.

## 4. Pitch: drop-sample aliasing changes with every setting

A 1 kHz tone through PITCH mode (live drop-sample read heads). *Spur* = everything except the pitched tone (+-200 Hz) and DC, relative to the tone. At large intervals part of it is the 79 ms splice of the live-stream read heads (sidebands near the tone); REPLAY, the hardware-faithful mode, has no splice.

|---|---|---|---|
| -12 | 500.0 Hz | -12.1 dB | 13500 Hz |
| -7 | 667.4 Hz | -9.8 dB | 8050 Hz |
| -5 | 749.2 Hz | -31.8 dB | 5700 Hz |
| +0 | 1000.0 Hz | -76.0 dB | 6800 Hz |

At +7 st the drop-sample reads produce **17.4 dB** more spurious energy than interpolated reads (ALIAS BYPASS).

## 5. REPLAY
A decaying 180 Hz hit lasts **0.047 s** at +7 st, **0.366 s** at 0 and **0.500 s** at -8 st (tuned up the sample plays faster and ends sooner; down it is slower and longer).

## 6. Level DAC
A held DC level decaying (300 ms) falls in **2** discrete steps between 250 and 300 ms: the 8-bit level DAC.

## 9. Bypass checks

- Analog stages off: the 18 kHz -> 8.04 kHz sampling alias stays at **-1.6 dB**.
- Digital stages off: no sampling alias (**-225.1 dB**), but the analog stages still colour the signal (THD **1.63 %** with DRIVE +18 dB).

## 11. Component variation
At 0 % left and right are identical. At 100 % the two voices differ slightly (peak difference 0.0108 on a -6 dBFS 5 kHz tone through Out 5-6).

## 12. Transients
A synthetic kick's crest factor: **7.8 dB** in, **7.9 dB** out (defaults, Out 7-8): the path does not compress.
